Tie-Complete Nearest-Signature Transfer for Depth-One QAOA

The estimator includes every training label tied at the selected neighbor radius, eliminating dependence on row serialization.

On row-held-out Gset neighborhoods, tie completion reduces mean angle MSE from 0.00137 to 0.00072. On collision-free signature folds, it matches ordinary nearest neighbors at MSE 0.00780 and relative regret 0.00267. The difference identifies duplicate handling rather than extrapolative gain.

The package authenticates the graph bytes, reconstructs all 48 QAOA landscapes, verifies zero signature overlap, tests permutation invariance, and regenerates every displayed artifact.
